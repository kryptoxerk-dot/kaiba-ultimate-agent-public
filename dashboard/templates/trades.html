LIVE AND PAPER

Trades

Paper prices are not market prices yet. Paper fills and marks are DexScreener quotes, which lag the real pool by about 45 s and show the old bonding-curve price just after a migration. Priced from the pool itself, every lane loses: a 60 s exit after migration is about −16% per trade (n=89, 2026-10-18 research). Treat every paper and exit-twin "pass" below as unproven until paper is priced from the pool.

Returns are realized, after costs, per position. Means cap each trade at +300% (the paper report's rule), and paper results leave out exactly what the daily report leaves out: trades opened before the honest cutoff, trades off sol whose entry was not priced from the pool or curve, and the trades distorted by the 10-18 placeholder native price. Paper trades are simulated fills: a paper win is a reason to test live, not money.
