Research labs

What real positions and real wallets show

Two studies that run every day on the box and trade nothing. The LP lab follows a frozen panel of Meteora DLMM pools and the LPs in them, and measures what their positions earned against simply holding. The wallet lab profiles how each tracked Solana S/A/B wallet trades. A profile describes a wallet; it is not evidence that following it pays. That question has its own pre-registered test, shown below.
