Owner: Codex, SOL-METHOD-LAB-20261103. This reference extends the existing
research skill; it grants no trade, signing, credential or provider authority.

## Run inside the existing allowance

The actual Grok routine is still `Crypto X alpha scan`, every 15 minutes.
Its saved method extension permits at most two of the existing eight X calls
on the first eligible run and then every fourth scheduled run. Use spare calls
only; preserve the required alpha searches and never add a second collector or
manual sweep. Read actual usage first: unknown allowance, weekly usage at or
above 80%, or fewer than 100 X reads remaining defers methods with a reason.
Account-wide limits and cooldowns apply. No new API spend or Alchemy calls.

The method search has its own 30-day/evergreen window. A useful old method is
not fresh token alpha. Do not rewind the alpha successful-start checkpoint or
replace its Latest searches. Record actual search order, retrieval time and
tool path. Use original posts and failure journals, not only profitable stories.

## Retain a reproducible hypothesis

For each original source preserve:

- Permalink, publication, provider retrieval and observation times; author and
  original versus quoted/reposted source; exact limited evidence excerpts.
- Entry trigger, invalidation/stop, exit, timeframe, holding period and cooldown.
- Necessary data and their observation times: pool-wide versus wallet-selected
  trades, executable bid/ask or routes, circulating cap versus FDV, pool identity.
- Fees, latency, execution size, inventory/capital requirements and failure cases.
- Author incentives, holdings, paid promotion and unverified performance claims.
- Explicit unknowns, contradictions and the next check that could falsify it.

Do not fill missing thresholds from imagination. An unspecified swap-count
baseline is unknown. A chart screenshot, vendor trade log or source wallet's
PnL does not verify a follower's returns. Market cap alone proves neither pool
depth nor a profitable exit. Preserve failed, dead and unsellable tokens.

action `model_verify_primary_source`. This is a research queue, not a trading
signal or an automatically admitted strategy. Claims remain unverified.
If the running producer cannot encode the contract, hold the method locally

actual connection needs a genuine signed receipt and matching processed row.

## Hand off to the bounded method lab

Catalogue ideas broadly, evaluate only pre-registered finite batches. Include
all tried variants in the trial ledger. Freeze rules, source hashes, eligible
population, feature clocks, capital/cost model, baselines and pass criteria
before seeing evaluation outcomes. Use untouched future data for changed rules.
Missing filter membership cannot silently remove a losing token. An empty
coverage set cannot vouch for previously saved positive returns.

The owner's requested 0.3/0.5/1 SOL tickets are research scenarios. They need
size-specific entry AND exit economics, realistic delays and finite cash;
do not multiply small paper returns into larger executable gains. Confidence
scores and smart-wallet counts do not authorize larger sizes. Existing risk,
exit priority, reconciliation, withdrawal controls and lead ownership remain.

Journal source -> hypothesis -> data readiness -> frozen trial -> result ->
decision. Keep rejected hypotheses and failed trials. A new model explanation,
more X coverage, or more backtest combinations is not evidence of better EV.
